What it will and will not do

Asks first

Before anything personal is used, you are shown the choice, and both options stay on screen long enough to read.

Waits for you

It prepares a next step and holds. It does not order, buy or commit anything on your behalf.

Explains itself

Every suggestion comes with a plain reason. You can ask why, and you can ask a person.

Works for small businesses too

The same assistant helps a shop owner keep track of what needs attention, without adding a new system to learn.
