Where people's needs become ideas that are listened to, tested and improved. The Lab is both a place and a method.

What the Lab actually does

Not a showcase. A working floor where ideas get tried against the way people live and work in India.

Starts with a person

Every project begins with someone's problem, described in their own words, not with a technology looking for a use.

Builds in weeks

Small teams, working prototypes, short cycles. Ideas that do not survive contact with users are stopped early.

Tests with users

Shopkeepers, care teams, engineers and consumers try the prototype before anything is scaled.

Governs before launch

Nothing reaches customers without passing the Responsible AI gates.

Who works here

The Lab runs as a joint floor: Vi's AI Office and engineering teams alongside technology and consulting partners, with specialists brought in for language, voice and infrastructure work.

Work with the Lab

We take a small number of new problems each quarter, from teams inside Vi, from enterprise customers, and from startups building for Indian users.

Bring us a problem
