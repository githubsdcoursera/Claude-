GRACIE

Vi's Responsible AI framework. Six pillars that turn principles into a process people can question and understand.

  • GovernanceClear policies and standards for AI at Vi, with defined decision rights.
  • ResponsibilityAssigns ownership for every AI service and its outcomes.
  • AccountabilityConsequences are traceable to a named owner, not to a system.
  • ComplianceMeets regulatory, contractual and internal obligations.
  • IntegrityTests for fairness, bias and quality before and after launch.
  • EthicsAligns AI with Vi's values and with the people it affects.
