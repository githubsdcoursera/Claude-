# Vi Clause Library (v1.0 DRAFT — Vi Legal to approve)

Standard and fallback wording used by the skills. IDs are referenced from the playbooks. All text is draft for Vi Legal.

## NDA

**CL-N2 Mutuality (fallback):** "Each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other, and the obligations in this Agreement apply to each Party as a Recipient."

**CL-N4 Definition:** "'Confidential Information' means all information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party, in any form, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure."

**CL-N5 Exclusions (delete residuals):** delete any clause permitting use of information retained in unaided memory; replace with standard exclusions: "(a) is or becomes public other than through breach; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure, as shown by written records; (c) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or (d) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction."

**CL-N6 Compelled disclosure:** "The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, court order or any governmental or regulatory authority (including the Department of Telecommunications and the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India), provided that, where lawful, it gives the Discloser prompt notice and discloses only what is required."

**CL-N8 Survival:** "The obligations in this Agreement survive for three (3) years after its expiry or termination, and, for trade secrets and personal data, for so long as the information remains confidential or personal data."

**CL-N9 Personal data:** "No personal data shall be disclosed under this Agreement unless the Parties first agree terms complying with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Personal data shall not be transferred outside India without the Discloser's prior written approval."

**CL-N11 Remedies:** "Each Party acknowledges that damages may not be an adequate remedy for breach and that the Discloser may seek injunctive relief, without prejudice to other remedies." (Delete liquidated damages.)

**CL-N13/N14 Law & disputes:** "This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute shall be finally resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 by a sole arbitrator appointed by mutual agreement. The seat and venue shall be Mumbai and the language English."

**CL-N15 Non-solicit (fallback only):** "For twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, neither Party shall actively solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom it had material contact under this Agreement, except through general advertisements not targeted at such employees." Delete any non-compete, exclusivity, standstill or licence.

**CL-N16 Publicity:** "Neither Party shall make any public announcement concerning this Agreement or use the other Party's name, trademarks or logos without its prior written consent."

## MoU

**CL-M-NB Non-binding:** "This MoU records the Parties' intentions and is not legally binding, except Clauses [Confidentiality], [Publicity], [Non-Exclusivity], [Data Protection], [Costs], [Term and Termination], [Governing Law and Dispute Resolution] and [No Partnership], which are binding. Any binding commitment on commercial terms will be set out in a definitive agreement signed by authorised representatives of both Parties."

**CL-M-EX Non-exclusivity:** "This MoU is non-exclusive. Nothing in it restricts either Party from entering into similar arrangements with any third party."

**CL-M-DATA Data:** "Vi data shared under this MoU remains the property of Vi, shall be stored and processed in India, shall be used only for the agreed use cases, and shall not be used to train, fine-tune or improve any model of the Partner or any third party without Vi's prior written approval."

**CL-M-RAI Responsible AI:** "The Partner shall comply with Vi's Responsible AI principles and stage-gate process (GRACIE) for all AI components used in the agreed use cases, including disclosure of models used, human oversight, testing for accuracy and bias, documentation, cooperation with Vi Responsible AI reviews and prompt reporting of AI incidents."

**CL-M-LAB AI Lab routing:** "All proofs of concept and solutions under this MoU shall be developed through the Vi AI Lab under the governance of Vi's AI Office."

**CL-M-COM Commercial principles:** "The Parties will agree commercial terms for each use case in a definitive agreement or under existing agreements between them. Pending that, each Party bears its own costs."

## Vendor / AI

**CL-V9-COND Conditional RAI:** "If the Vendor uses any artificial intelligence or machine-learning system in performing the Services, Annex [RAI] shall apply from the first such use, and the Vendor shall notify Vi in writing before that use."

**CL-V7 Residency:** "The Vendor shall store and process Vi Data only in India unless Vi gives prior written approval."

**CL-V8 Incidents:** "The Vendor shall notify Vi of any Security Incident within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it and shall provide all cooperation Vi reasonably requires, including to meet any reporting obligation to CERT-In or any other authority."
